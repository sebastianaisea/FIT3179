Building the World of LEGO

75 Years of Sets, Themes and Innovation (1949 – 2023)

A data visualisation exploring how LEGO sets have evolved over time, which themes have shaped its history and its presence in Australia.

Introduction

From a simple wooden toy to a global phenomenon, LEGO has inspired creativity for generations.

This visualisation explores LEGO sets, themes, complexity and geographic presence over time, with a focus on questions relevant to an Australian audience.

Key questions

  • How has the number of LEGO sets changed over time?
  • Are LEGO sets getting bigger?
  • Which themes have been most popular?
  • How has LEGO catered to different ages?
  • Where are LEGO stores located in Australia?

Data sources

  • LEGO sets and themes dataset
    Current project CSV (1949–2023): set names, release years, piece counts and themes.
  • Australian LEGO store locations
    Planned source for store names, addresses and coordinates.
  • ABS population data
    Planned source for comparisons of stores per person.
