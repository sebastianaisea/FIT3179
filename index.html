The Evolution of LEGO

Exploring LEGO's growth and changing product range from 1949 to 2023.

1. The Growth of LEGO Sets

This visualisation shows the total number of LEGO sets released each year from 1949 to 2023. It highlights how LEGO's product catalogue has evolved over time, revealing periods of rapid growth and changes in release activity.

2. Are LEGO Sets Getting Bigger?

This box plot compares the distribution of LEGO piece counts across different decades, from the 1950s to the 2020s. Boxes show the middle 50% of piece counts, with a dark line marking the median. Whiskers reach the smallest and largest observed counts within 1.5 times the interquartile range (IQR) of the box. Outlier points are hidden to keep typical set sizes readable, but all positive piece counts contribute to the quartiles and median. Hover for summary statistics, hidden outlier counts and the largest set. Sets with missing or zero piece counts are excluded; the 2020s covers 2020?2023 only.
