# Australian state and territory boundaries

Source: https://github.com/rowanhogan/australian-states

Downloaded from https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rowanhogan/australian-states/master/states.geojson on 2026-10-14. The repository describes the boundaries as simplified from ABS data. All eight original features and coordinates are preserved. Joins use properties.STATE_NAME and the CSV state_name. The ACT locator is at Canberra (149.13, -35.28); it does not represent a store location or count.
